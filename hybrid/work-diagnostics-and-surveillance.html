    Diagnostics and Surveillance

    Strengthening the information and testing systems needed to detect threats earlier and respond faster.

    In this portfolio

    AI in health

    Works with

    Ministry of Health and Family Welfare and state governments

    The challenge

    The COVID‑19 pandemic demonstrated how quickly public‑health response can be constrained when surveillance data are delayed, fragmented or incomplete. It also left India with a much larger molecular‑testing footprint, creating an opportunity to use existing infrastructure more efficiently for other priority diseases.

    Stronger preparedness therefore requires two things at once: surveillance systems that can detect unusual patterns early and diagnostics that are affordable, adaptable and connected to programme action. That means better data integration, stronger private‑sector reporting, trained teams, responsive governance and diagnostic pathways that fit real‑world public‑health settings.

    Our approach

    Detect signals earlier

    Support threshold‑based early‑warning systems, dashboards and structured reviews that help surveillance teams identify unusual patterns and trigger timely field investigation.

    Improve completeness and timeliness of reporting

    Strengthen public- and private‑sector reporting through simpler workflows, provider engagement and capacity building so surveillance platforms receive more decision‑useful data.

    Build institutional capacity

    Support governance, workforce development, outbreak‑response tools and integration into existing platforms so stronger surveillance practices can continue within government systems.

    Use diagnostic infrastructure more effectively

    Map molecular‑testing capacity, define feasible public‑health use cases and support regulatory and market analysis for adaptable diagnostic platforms that can serve multiple disease priorities.

    Our impact

    • EARLY WARNING3.4 crore people covered

      EWARS operated across 20 Madhya Pradesh districts and generated 342 alerts. District surveillance units responded to all alerts, with field investigation confirming 26 outbreaks.

    • PRIVATE‑SECTOR SURVEILLANCE118 facilities onboarded to IHIP

      A focused engagement model improved routine reporting, including a 138% increase in laboratory‑form reporting and a 52% increase in reporting days per week.

    • NATIONAL SURVEILLANCE TOOLS5 dashboards scaled nationally

      Five thematic disease‑surveillance dashboards, first piloted in Madhya Pradesh, were subsequently scaled nationally on the Integrated Health Information Platform.

    • DIAGNOSTIC INFRASTRUCTURE~7,900 RT‑PCR platforms mapped

      A pan‑India mapping exercise assessed the installed RT‑PCR base and explored how molecular‑testing infrastructure could support diseases beyond COVID‑19, including tuberculosis.
